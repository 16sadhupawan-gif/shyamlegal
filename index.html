Profile

Pawan Shyam is an independent litigation and advisory practitioner in Bangalore. He is qualified as an Advocate in India and as an Attorney-at-Law in the State of Texas, U.S.A.

His practice spans education law, company law, insolvency, land law, civil litigation, and constitutional and administrative law. He appears before the Supreme Court of India, the High Court of Karnataka, the National Company Law Tribunal, trial courts and administrative authorities.

On the advisory side, he handles mergers and acquisitions, corporate structuring, regulatory compliance and succession planning for businesses in the education, construction and media production sectors. He also drafts and negotiates commercial contracts, shareholder and partnership agreements and wills for companies and individuals, including contracts governed by Indian and U.S. law.

He set up his independent practice in Bangalore in 2020 after completing his LL.M. at the University of Texas School of Law. He runs each matter himself, from strategy and pleadings through to hearings, and instructs senior counsel where a matter calls for it.

Property

Real Estate Law

For buyers, owners, developers and institutions.

He advises on acquiring, holding and developing property in Karnataka, and represents clients when title or possession is challenged. Transactional work starts with title verification. Disputes are assessed early for limitation and maintainability, which often decide them.

  • Title verification and due diligence
  • Sale deeds, agreements to sell and conveyancing
  • Joint development and lease agreements
  • Suits for declaration, injunction and partition
  • Challenges to sale deeds and limitation defences
  • RERA complaints and builder–buyer disputes
Disputes

Litigation & Dispute Resolution

From the trial court to the Supreme Court of India.

This is the core of the practice. He takes matters from strategy and drafting through to oral argument, and briefs senior counsel on higher-court matters where needed. He appears before the High Court of Karnataka, the NCLT, trial courts, arbitral tribunals and regulatory authorities.

  • Commercial and civil suits
  • Writ petitions and challenges to regulatory action
  • Insolvency proceedings under the IBC before the NCLT
  • Domestic arbitration and related court proceedings
  • Society, trust and election disputes
  • Appeals and Supreme Court matters
